Welcome to my new blog.

I will use this space to document what I learn from vulnerability research, reverse engineering, fuzzing, and firmware analysis. I want to capture not only the results, but also **how I framed each problem and tested each hypothesis**.

## Written in two languages

Whenever possible, every post will be available in both Korean and English. Use the language switch in the header to open the matching translation of the post you are reading.

```text
observe → hypothesize → verify → document
```

I will also move and refine my existing Tistory posts here over time.
