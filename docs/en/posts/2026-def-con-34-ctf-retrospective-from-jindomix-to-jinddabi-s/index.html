hello. 

This is ZZoMb1E from Jinddabi’s, who advanced to the DEF CON 34 CTF finals in 2026.

DEF CON CTF is one of the most prestigious and largest hacking competitions in the world. If you just search on Google, you will find something like the following.

This competition is considered a dream stage for many people studying hacking. Since 2018, when I first became interested in the security field, I have made it my goal to advance to the DEF CON CTF finals for about 8 years. Of course, preparations began in earnest only after 2023, when I returned to school.

Actually, I didn’t plan on participating in this DEF CON from the beginning. It all started with a call from our juniors asking us to participate together in the Codegate CTF held before DEF CON.

Jindomix team formation process

I briefly summarized the conversation I had with my junior.

In response to the suggestion to participate while having fun, I contacted people in the field around me to ask if they would like to join, and as they invited their friends again, the size of the team naturally grew. The team that was created that way is Jindomix.

After Codegate, a suggestion was made in the Discord room: “Let’s participate in DEF CON as is.” With this, full-fledged preparations began, and later, alliances with other hacking teams were formed, and the current Jinddabi’s was born.

I never dreamed that my junior’s simple suggestion, which was no different from usual, would become this big. However, as the team was formed and preparations progressed, I began to think that this could really be the opportunity to achieve the goal I had longed for. (Until now, I had practically given up on AI.)

It was also possible to participate in the finals online from Seoul.

However, since DEF CON was a stage I had dreamed of for a long time, I wanted to participate in person in Las Vegas, where the event was held. So I decided to fly to the local area, even if it was a bit difficult.

Ahead of the competition, we developed an automation tool and compiled helpful materials in advance, but it didn’t seem to be as helpful as expected during the finals. Still, I tried to do everything I could during the three days of the competition.

The routine at the time was roughly like this. I participated in the competition from morning until 6pm, and after the day’s schedule was over, I set a goal to further analyze the problem and organize it by the next day. Afterwards, I fell asleep for a while and woke up again at dawn to develop automation tools and organize data. Then, I went back to sleep around 4 a.m., woke up in the morning, and immediately participated in the competition.

The finals were divided into three roles: Attack, Defense, and KoTH. At first, I worked on the Attack team, but I decided that it was important to secure a high rank in KoTH in order to improve the overall ranking, so I joined KoTH partway through. In the Attack team, juniors gathered in Seoul to solve problems together, and I felt that I could trust them enough to leave it to them, so I focused on KoTH from then on.

I know that many of the participants either followed a similar schedule or participated in the competition with very little sleep.

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to everyone who worked together and worked hard for this competition. Even though it was the first joint team formed, thanks to trusting in each other’s capabilities and doing our best, we were able to achieve the valuable result of 7th place in the DEF CON CTF final stage that we had dreamed of.

I never expected that the team that started with a single contact from an ordinary junior would progress through Codegate and all the way to the DEF CON finals. For me, it was a moment of achieving a goal I had longed for, and it was an even more meaningful experience because we were together. Once again, thank you to everyone who joined us on this journey.

I was able to spend a meaningful time saying hello to not only my team but also many people from other teams. As much as I achieved a goal I had longed for, the fact that I was able to meet and build relationships with good people through this opportunity meant a lot to me.

I hope that everyone who joins us will have only good things in their future, and we sincerely thank you again. See you next time at a better location.
